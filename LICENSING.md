## Attribution (tier 2)

When reusing CC BY-SA 4.0 material, credit:
“Ekkehard Finkeissen — Finkeissen Research Program (https://github.com/finkeissen)”
and indicate changes. Derivatives must be shared under the same licence.

## Policy

- Local experimental runs and unreleased evidence remain private until
  deliberately published under the licence of the target repository.
- Full book volumes remain with the author (print/PDF); repositories carry
  teasers only, except works never published as books (bootloader, dentistry),
  which appear complete there but remain all rights reserved.
- Statements on licences in text or manifests never override this map;
  the repository-level LICENSE files are the operative documents.
